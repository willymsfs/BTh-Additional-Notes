# St Francis de Sales — Course Home

> [!info] Method
> Spiritual-affective and pastoral — doctrine applied to the ordering of desire, the same family as Owen's affective divinity. Audit: [[12 - Method Audit of My Courses#8. St Francis de Sales|method audit §8]].


> [!quote] The motto
> **Vive Jésus! — Live Jesus!**
> *"To die to all other love in order to live to Jesus' love… I love Jesus, Who lives and reigns for ever and ever. Amen."* — *Treatise on the Love of God*, Bk XII

**François de Sales** (21 Aug 1567 – 28 Dec 1622), Bishop of Geneva resident at Annecy, canonised 1665, **Doctor of the Church 1877**, patron of Catholic writers and journalists (1923). Called the **Doctor of Charity** and the **Gentleman Saint**.

His one great thesis, which reorganised Catholic spirituality: **holiness is not a monastic monopoly. Devotion belongs to every state of life, and it is made not of austerity but of love.**

## Competency checklist

- [ ] Date and narrate the life in six movements (Savoy → Paris → Padua → Chablais → Annecy → Lyons)
- [ ] Explain the **Paris crisis of predestination** (1586–87) and why it is the seed of the whole doctrine
- [ ] Define **devotion** in his exact terms and distinguish it from four false devotions
- [ ] Lay out the **five parts** of the *Introduction* and the **twelve books** of the *Treatise*
- [ ] Distinguish **signified will** / **will of good pleasure**, and **resignation** / **holy indifference**
- [ ] Explain **ecstasy of act and life** as superior to ecstasy of prayer
- [ ] Reproduce and interpret five Salesian images (wax ball, statue in the niche, child at the breast, deaf lutenist, bees)
- [ ] State the apologetic argument *"Who sent you?"* and its charitable tone
- [ ] Situate him in relation to Trent, to later rigorism/Jansenism, and to *Lumen Gentium* 39–42
- [ ] Name the Salesian congregations with correct founders and dates (Visitation 1610; MSFS 1838, Mermier; SDB 1859, Bosco)
- [ ] State his sacramental discipline: frequency of Mass, communion, confession, examen, annual retreat
- [ ] Explain the rule for **great** vs **small** temptations, and the three marks of a true inspiration
- [ ] Quote the maxim on disquietude and explain why anxiety is a spiritual, not merely psychological, problem
- [ ] Describe the Visitation coat of arms and the Salesian heart-tradition behind the Sacred Heart devotion
- [ ] Compare **holy indifference** with **prapatti / śaraṇāgati** after Clooney

## Quick reference — the four master works

| Work | Date | Addressee | Genre | One line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *The Catholic Controversy* | 1594–98 leaflets, later collected | Calvinists of the Chablais | Apologetics | Faith has a rule, and preachers need a mission |
| *Introduction to the Devout Life* | 1609 | **Philothea** ("lover of God") | Ascetical manual | Devotion in the world, for everyone |
| *Treatise on the Love of God* | 1616 | **Theotimus** ("honouring God") | Mystical theology | The whole life of charity, from spark to ecstasy |
| *Letters* (2,000+) | lifelong | named souls | Spiritual direction | The workshop from which the books came |
